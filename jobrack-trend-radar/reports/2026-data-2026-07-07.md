## 3. 2026 market data (published this year, shapes the niche's talking points)

**Near, 2026 State of LatAm Hiring Report** ([report](https://www.hirewithnear.com/state-of-latam-hiring-report), [press release](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/885984925/near-releases-2026-state-of-latam-hiring-report-as-demand-for-software-engineers-surges-250)):
2,000+ placements analyzed across 411 roles; demand for LatAm software
engineers up 250% YoY; 84% of placements mid-level or senior; speed-to-hire
and talent quality cited above cost as motivations; typical fills in 7-28 days
vs 3-6 months US; $35k+ saved annually per hire. Companion release:
[sales/SDR roles are the #1 demand driver at 22%](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/921811244/sales-roles-are-the-no-1-driver-of-u-s-companies-hiring-in-latin-america-hire-with-near-data-shows).
Near also publishes a [2026 US vs LatAm salary guide](https://www.hirewithnear.com/salary-guide-us-vs-latam).

**2026 offshore rate benchmarks** (developer/agency roles, hourly, from
[Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/offshore-staffing-cost-statistics-2026),
[Aalpha](https://www.aalpha.net/articles/offshore-software-development-hourly-rates/),
[Insignia](https://www.insigniaresource.com/research/outsourcing-rates-by-country/),
[Kore BPO](https://korebpo.com/offshore-developer-cost-by-country-2026/)):
Eastern Europe $35-70; LatAm $30-60 (Mexico mid $38-52, senior $52-68);
Philippines $15-42; India $18-45. Trend lines: Eastern Europe rates up 10-15%
since 2022 (Romania and Bulgaria absorbing former Ukraine demand); Philippines
IT-BPM wages rising 4-5% per year; India at 9.5% salary inflation. The
40-70% savings narrative still holds but the floor is rising, which
strengthens quality-and-retention arguments over pure cost arguments.
Note: these are content-marketing publications by offshore vendors, useful as
the numbers competitors will quote, not as neutral statistics.
